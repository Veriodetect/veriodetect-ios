HOW TO READ THIS

Methodology & limits

M0 is a weighted experimental evidence index. Its weights and thresholds are engineering hypotheses and are not calibrated human/AI probabilities.

M1.1 searches for the largest M0 change and uses up to 100 within-conversation no-handoff surrogates to estimate a permutation p-value.

Interpretation: a low p-value supports the existence of an unusual behavioural transition. It does not prove that an AI agent took control.

Conversation text is not stored in local history. Detailed evidence stores only derived statistics.

History

About VerioDetect

Experimental evidence, not proof

VerioDetect analyses observable conversation timing and behavioural patterns. It does not identify a person and it cannot prove that a counterparty is an AI agent.

M0 is the frozen temporal evidence index. M1.1 tests the strongest behavioural transition against within-conversation no-handoff surrogates. Short extreme transitions may be surfaced without a p-value when there is insufficient data for M1.1.

Privacy: analysis is performed in your browser on this device. Conversation text is not written to local history.
