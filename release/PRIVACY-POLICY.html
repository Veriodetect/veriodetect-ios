What VerioDetect does

VerioDetect analyses timing and behavioural patterns in conversations and X activity. Its experimental results are indicators, not proof of identity, AI control or automation. Live X access is optional and requires manual approval. You can analyse locally available imported data without registering for live access.

Conversation and imported-data analysis

Conversation text and imported X files are parsed and analysed on your device. That analysis code does not upload conversation text or imported files to our servers. The app saves up to 30 conversation-analysis summaries locally, including participant labels and derived statistics, rather than the original conversation text. You can remove those records using Clear in History.

Retrieved or imported post text is not written to saved analysis history. Input and results may remain in the current screen while the app is running. Information stored on your device may be included in device backups according to your Apple/device settings.

Requesting live X access

When you request live access, our backend receives your name, email address and optional reason, together with a request identifier, request time and review status. An administrator reviews the request. Approval is not automatic and creates an individual access code with a daily allowance and, where configured, an expiry date.

The server stores a SHA-256 hash of the access code and associates its access and usage records with your registration. The app stores your individual code locally for reuse. We do not put our administrator or X API credentials in the app.

At registration, the app generates a private deletion receipt and saves it on your device before submitting your request. Its hash is stored with the server record. Keep your receipt privately: it lets you delete that request even if approval has not been granted. If you have several requests, the app can keep separate receipts for them.

Retrieving public X activity

When you choose Fetch, the app sends the requested public X handle and your individual access code to our backend over HTTPS. The backend queries X and returns a sample of up to 100 recent activities, including post IDs, text and timestamps. Analysis then runs on your device. The retrieval code does not deliberately save returned post text to the access database.

The service keeps per-code daily usage counters and applies an additional IP-based limit to control costs and misuse. A retrieval attempt may consume a quota unit even if it fails. Checking your access code does not consume a live-retrieval quota unit.

Purposes and legal bases

We process registration and access information to respond to your request for the optional live service, review access and provide that service. Where the GDPR applies, this processing is based on taking steps at your request and providing the requested service. Limited usage/security processing supports our legitimate interests in preventing misuse, protecting the service and controlling API costs. Information you choose to send to support is used to handle your enquiry. We may also process information where necessary to meet a legal obligation.

Do not put unnecessary sensitive personal information in the optional reason field or support messages. Provide only conversation or activity data you are entitled to analyse.

Service providers and international processing

We use Netlify for the website, hosting and backend functions, and Upstash Redis for registration, access and quota records. X supplies public-account activity. These providers process the requests required to deliver their services, including network information such as IP addresses and operational logs.

Provider infrastructure can process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Applicable processor terms and transfer safeguards govern that processing. You can ask us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data. Provider information is available from Netlify, Upstash and X.

Retention

We keep registration and access records until you request deletion; these records do not have an automatic inactivity expiry. Revoking or expiring a code stops access but does not itself erase the registration. Daily quota keys expire after approximately two days, and duplicate-request guards after one day.

Local analysis history is capped at 30 records and can be cleared in the app. Clearing History alone does not remove your server registration or saved access code. Provider operational logs and backups follow their retention schedules and any applicable legal requirements; they are separate from the live records removed by the app’s deletion flow.

Delete your access and data

In the app, open About → Delete my access and data. Enter your individual access code or select a saved deletion receipt, tick the acknowledgement and confirm. An approved access code, including an expired or revoked code, deletes registration/access records for its approved email. A deletion receipt removes only the particular request it belongs to, including access subsequently granted to that request.

After the server confirms deletion, the app clears saved analysis history and the stored access code on that device. Receipts for other requests are kept so those requests can still be deleted. Deletion removes the related live registration, access-code hash, receipt mapping and quota records. It does not remove data independently held by X or another device’s local data, and does not instantly purge provider backups or security logs.

If you have lost your code/receipt, are using an older version without the deletion controls, or need help with your information, contact info@cquestra.com. We may need to verify your identity to protect your records. Normal in-app deletion does not require an email or support conversation.

Clipboard and sharing

The app reads clipboard text when you choose Paste. Choosing Share opens the iOS share sheet. Anything you send is handled by the destination you choose under its own privacy practices. Avoid sharing other people’s private information without permission.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include an advertising SDK or cross-app tracking integration. We do not use the app’s registration data for advertising. This website includes no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, registration forms or browser-storage functionality. Ordinary hosting connection and security logs can still be processed.
